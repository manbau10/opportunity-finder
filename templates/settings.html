Connect your AI key

You do not need to understand APIs. Choose where your key came from, paste it once, then use Save and test. Keys are encrypted before storage and are never returned to your browser.

Recommended for your Poe key

Set up Poe in three steps

  1. Select Poe below.
  2. Paste the key from Poe API keys. Do not paste your Poe password.
  3. Press Save and test. The endpoint and recommended model are filled automatically.

Poe uses your Poe points for API requests. Use an official Poe bot and check your points before generating a large application workspace.

Worldwide job coverage

Use Google results through Serper

DuckDuckGo requires no key, but cloud-hosted searches can return limited job results. For country-by-country discovery, select Serper, create a separate search key at serper.dev, paste it above, and press Save and test.

The search key is separate from your Poe AI key. Worldwide mode searches the candidate's target title across 24 markets. Preferred locations entered under CVs replace the default market list.

Supported providers

AI: Poe, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral, Ollama, LM Studio and HTTPS OpenAI-compatible endpoints.

Search: DuckDuckGo without a key, or Tavily, Brave Search and Serper with your key.

Local Ollama and LM Studio endpoints work only when this application runs on the same computer and ALLOW_LOCAL_AI=true. A hosted Render service cannot connect to an API running on your laptop.
