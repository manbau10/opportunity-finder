Provider configuration

Keys are encrypted before storage and are never returned to your browser. Free and paid providers can use the same application-pack workflow.

Supported providers

AI: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral, Ollama, LM Studio and HTTPS OpenAI-compatible endpoints.

Search: DuckDuckGo without a key, or Tavily, Brave Search and Serper with your key.

Local Ollama and LM Studio endpoints work only when this application runs on the same computer and ALLOW_LOCAL_AI=true. A hosted Render service cannot connect to an API running on your laptop.
